02

The Mark

The brandmark is a stylised takeoff: layered motion blades and an origami dart breaking upward. It is the most important asset and must stay recognisable at any size.

The WingSlim leading blade — the contrail / horizon line.
The SwooshBold hooked stroke — momentum and lift.
The DartFolded paper-plane nose — the journey itself.

05

Clear Space & Minimum Size

Clear space

Keep a corridor of 1× — the height of the icon (x) — free on every side. No type, image edge or other logo may enter it.

↕ 1x corridor

Minimum sizes

Below these thresholds legibility breaks down. Use the simplified favicon under 32 px.

Full lockup
Desktop nav · ≥ 132 px wide
Lockup — mobile
Mobile navbar · ≥ 96 px wide
Icon
Never below 24 px tall
Favicon
32 px & 16 px
Don’t: recolour the mark, add gradients or shadows, stretch the lockup, swap the wordmark font, or move the orange off the dart and the i-dot. Orange is a spark, never a fill.
